🌐 WebXR AR App

How to Use:

  1. Enter AR: Tap the "Enter AR" button below
  2. Scan Environment: Move your device to scan surfaces
  3. Look for Reticle: A ring appears on detected surfaces
  4. Select Model: Choose from Building, Floor Plan, or Equipment
  5. Place Model: Tap the screen to place the selected model
  6. Multiple Placements: Place as many models as you want!

Requirements: Android device with ARCore support, Chrome 79+ or Samsung Internet
