🌐 WebXR AR App

How to Use:

  1. Enter AR: Tap the "Enter AR" button below
  2. Scan Environment: Move your device (Android: detects surfaces, iPhone: places at fixed distance)
  3. Look for Reticle: A blue/purple ring shows placement location
  4. Select Model: Choose from Building, Floor Plan, or Equipment
  5. Place Model: Tap the screen to place the selected model
  6. Multiple Placements: Place as many models as you want!

Best on: Android with ARCore (Chrome 79+)

iPhone: Use Mozilla WebXR Viewer or IQ3Connect XR app (fallback mode: places 1m ahead)
